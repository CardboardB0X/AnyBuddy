How it began

Early May 2026

The Spark ⚡

A small group of friends in CvSU realized everyone kept asking each other for everyday favors but there was no safe, trustworthy platform to get connected.

Mid May 2026

Building the Engine 🛠️

They designed a custom companion marketplace that prioritizes trust, reviews, safe payment, and complete user freedom.

Late May 2026

Launch Day 🚀

AnyBuddy launched officially across the Philippines, opening doors for real human connections and companionship.

What we believe

🤝

Human Connection

We believe companionship should feel natural and warm, not transactional or awkward. Real people, real moments.

🛡️

Trust & Safety

💪

Empowerment

Today & Beyond

AnyBuddy is still young—we launched in May 2026—but our community is already growing across the Philippines. From event stand-ins and creative collaborators to listeners and security companions, Buddies are showing up for real moments in real people’s lives.

This project was built as a student initiative to explore how technology can make companionship and help more accessible. We are learning every day, and we are grateful you are here at the beginning.
